# ARCHE Product Overview

## Purpose

ARCHE is an attention technology for intelligent systems.

Its purpose is to help determine where limited cognitive resources should be directed as conditions, priorities, and consequences change.

## The Attention Problem

Intelligence operates under constraints.

No system has unlimited time, context, compute, or model capacity. As autonomous systems take on larger responsibilities, they must do more than possess intelligence: they must allocate it effectively.

Without a dedicated attention capability, expensive cognition can be spent on low-value work while consequential conditions receive too little attention or receive it too late.

ARCHE is being developed to address that problem directly.

## Product Position

ARCHE treats attention as its own systems problem.

It is concerned with prioritization of cognitive effort rather than the generation of evidence, execution of work, or exercise of authority. This gives intelligent applications a dedicated capability for deciding what deserves cognitive resources without requiring attention policy to be buried inside every application prompt.

## Intended Outcomes

ARCHE is intended to support intelligent systems that are more capable of:

- concentrating effort where it has the greatest value;
- responding to changing priorities;
- recognizing when unresolved matters deserve greater attention;
- considering potential consequence to the user;
- reducing unnecessary use of expensive cognition; and
- escalating cognitive effort when circumstances warrant it.

## Relationship to AI Models

ARCHE is not an AI model and is not intended to replace one.

Models provide intelligence. ARCHE addresses where and when that intelligence should be applied.

This distinction allows model capability and attention management to evolve independently.

## Relationship to ARCHETRON

ARCHE is part of ARCHETRON, the technology ecosystem built by VOLSHi for autonomous and intelligent systems.

Within ARCHETRON, major system responsibilities are separated rather than collapsing evidence, observation, governance, execution, and attention into a single opaque intelligence layer.

ARCHE owns the attention problem within that ecosystem.

## Development Status

ARCHE is under active development. Current work is focused on establishing and validating the foundation of the attention capability.

This public repository intentionally describes the product without publishing proprietary implementation architecture, internal algorithms, control logic, protocols, or reconstructive technical detail.
