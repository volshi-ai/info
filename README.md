# VOLSHI

**Infrastructure for autonomous intelligence.**

VOLSHI builds the span between machine intelligence and human experience.

Models are becoming more capable, more interchangeable, and easier to replace. The responsibilities surrounding intelligence are not. Evidence, learning, memory, attention, observation, authority, execution, and accountability have to persist even when the model changes.

VOLSHI develops the infrastructure that moves those responsibilities outside any single model.

> **Models provide intelligence. VOLSHI builds the infrastructure around it.**

This repository is the public information index for VOLSHI: the company, its systems, research direction, products, and technical work.

## ARCHETRON

**ARCHETRON is the VOLSHI technology ecosystem.**

It is the engineering territory where VOLSHI develops durable capabilities around machine intelligence: knowing, learning, temporal continuity, attention, observation, authority, execution, and accountable interaction with people.

ARCHETRON is not a model and is not a single application. Its systems are designed so critical state and responsibility can survive changes in model provider, model generation, runtime, and interface.

The public boundary is intentionally expressed in responsibilities and externally relevant guarantees rather than reconstructable implementation topology.

## Core Systems

### [ERIE](https://github.com/ArchePersona/ERIE-info)
**Knowing.** Evidence, provenance, structured knowledge, and evidence-grounded investigation outside the model.

### [ELLE](https://github.com/CenturionOversight/ELLE)
**Learning.** Developmental continuity and durable learning from experience independent of replaceable model backends.

### [ARCHE](https://github.com/ArchePersona/ARCHE-info)
**Attention.** Persistent allocation of cognitive effort according to pressure, consequence, and human cost.

### [CHRONOS](https://github.com/volshi-ai/chronos)
**Temporal continuity.** Preserves immutable history while allowing what the system knows about that history to evolve over time.

## Observation and Governance

### [PEEP](https://github.com/ArchePersona/PEEP-info)
**Execution observation.** An independent view of what execution actually did across operational surfaces.

### [RATTER](https://github.com/ArchePersona/RATTER-info)
**Operational telemetry.** An outside operational record that does not depend on an intelligent system's own account of its behavior.

### BOUNCER
**Governance.** Authority boundaries, permissions, and control over what intelligent systems are allowed to do.

## Public Architecture Boundary

These public repositories describe what each system is responsible for and the guarantees relevant outside the implementation boundary.

They are **not** intended to publish a reconstructable blueprint of ARCHETRON.

Internal topology, engine-to-engine exchange contracts, schemas, persistence mechanisms, orchestration policy, integrity mechanisms, proprietary algorithms, and implementation details remain private VOLSHI technology.
