# KATIE

**The record.** Knowledge Authority Time Indexing Engine.

KATIE is the tamper-evident, append-only knowledge record at the center of VOLSHI. Every fact it holds carries its birth stamp, its authority, and its provenance, so what the system believed when it acted can always be checked.

A thing without a KID is not a thing. The record is the hard drive part: append-only and permanent. The standing belief is the memory part: it rewrites with one append.

## Authority

Every entry carries an authority from 0 to 9. 9 is held for the user alone. The machine ceiling is 8.

Trust is graded, not assumed. The record shows what it relied on and how much it trusted it.

## Time

KATIE records the what, when, and why of machine justification. Time is a vector, not a stamp: when a fact was born, when it was learned, and when it changed are all part of the record.

## The live record

KATIE is served by the [TRIFACTA API](../trifacta/) at https://trifacta-api.onrender.com. Ask a question and the answer comes back with its proof: the KID, the birth stamp, the trust score, and the source.
